DaktarSetu Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DaktarSetu collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the DaktarSetu application and related services.

Information We Collect

DaktarSetu may collect information required to create and manage an account, authenticate users, provide clinic-management features, and operate the service. Depending on how the service is used, this may include account, contact, clinic, appointment, patient, and operational information.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide and maintain DaktarSetu, authenticate users, provide requested features, protect the service, communicate with users, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your DaktarSetu account through the account-deletion feature in the app or through our account deletion page.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Contact

For privacy questions or account-deletion requests, contact: support@daktarsetu.com
